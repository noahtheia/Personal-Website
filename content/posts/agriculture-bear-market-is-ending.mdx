## A consolidated supply chain feeding a fragmented field

*Draft — the input side has consolidated hard: the top three seed-and-chemical firms now account for roughly 60% of global commercial seed sales. The demand side — ~608 million farms worldwide — has no comparable pricing power. Value has migrated up the chain, away from the dirt.*

## Costs that won't come back down

*Draft — farm production expenses have compounded at about 6.4% a year since COVID, versus a 3–4% long-run norm — energy, logistics, labor, and now datacenter competition for power and water. Even if output prices do nothing, the cost curve alone forces a reset.*
