Hi — welcome.

This is where I'll write up investment ideas I'm working on. Most posts will follow
roughly the same shape:

1. **What's the situation.** A company, a setup, or a framework I'm thinking about.
2. **Why it might be interesting.** The thesis in plain language.
3. **What could go wrong.** The bear case, honestly weighted.
4. **The numbers.** Usually attached as an Excel file at the bottom of the post.

## On the spreadsheets

I think working through a spreadsheet is the fastest way to actually understand a
business. So most posts will come with the one I built — not because it's the
"right" answer, but because it lets you trace what I'm assuming and swap in your own
numbers.

Download the attached file, break it, argue with it. That's the point.

## A note on what this isn't

Nothing here is investment advice. I'm sharing my own thinking publicly because
writing it out forces me to be sharper. Use it as input to your own process.

If that sounds useful, **subscribe below** — you'll get an email each time I post a
new one. No other emails, ever.
